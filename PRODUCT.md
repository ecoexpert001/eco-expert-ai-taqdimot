## Product Purpose

A cinematic, step-by-step web presentation of ECO-EXPERT.AI, the artificial intelligence platform for ecological expertise that the Center's Artificial Intelligence Department is developing. It shows the platform's six AI agents at work, in the order of the expertise process (application stage, expert review stage, management oversight), and closes on ECO-MAP's picture of Uzbekistan. Success: the audience leaves believing the deck's three words in equal measure (faster, more accurate, more transparent) and trusting that the data stays on the Center's own servers. The equal weighting is carried over from the user's answer for ECO SERVICE.

## Positioning

ECO-EXPERT.AI is one platform of six AI agents covering every stage of the expertise process. ECO-INTAKE checks the application and project documents against the established requirements and prepares a list of deficiencies (90 seconds per application instead of 3–4 hours, 95% accuracy). ECO-CONSULT answers designers and applicants 24/7 in Uzbek, Russian and English, trained on Law No. ZRU-1036, Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 234 and other applicable regulations. ECO-COMPARE compares conclusions across stages and highlights mismatched figures and unfulfilled requirements (2 minutes per comparison). ECO-EXPERT COPILOT is a virtual assistant for experts based on laws, decrees, resolutions and ecology-related materials; it finds the right standard with its source. ECO-AUDIT identifies deficiencies and inconsistencies in conclusions and reports them to management (2 minutes per conclusion). ECO-MAP maps all companies' coordinates and identifies emissions by district, built on more than 180,000 conclusions being converted to digital format. The platform runs on-premise on the Center's own servers.
